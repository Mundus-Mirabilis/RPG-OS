Detailed Description

The worn/equipped gear of one sheet: one item per equipment slot.

Why slot -> itemId rather than a list of "equipped" flags?
The ruleset already declares the legal slots (equipment_slots); mapping slot id to item id is the direct transcription of "what am I wearing where". The engine validates that an item is actually allowed in a slot before calling equip; this type is the dumb storage underneath.
