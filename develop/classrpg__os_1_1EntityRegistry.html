Detailed Description

Owns the live entities and looks them up by stable EntityId.

This is the universal-mode entity pool: entities are created by the engine as std::shared_ptr, handed to the registry, and thereafter addressed by id (lookup), by handle (non-owning reference), or by iteration. It is the piece that lets an application answer "give me that character", "is this creature still alive", and "list everyone on the field" without keeping raw pointers itself.
