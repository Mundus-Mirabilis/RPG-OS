Detailed Description

Optional carrying / encumbrance rules.

Why a capacity formula and ratio levels?
Systems differ wildly in how much a hero can carry (D&D: 15 × STR pounds; BRP: encumbrance points; TDE: abstract). A formula keeps the limit data-driven, and a list of ratio ceilings (carried ÷ capacity) with an attached condition is the generic shape of "encumbered at 50%, heavily encumbered at 100%". A ruleset without an encumbrance section simply has enabled == false and the engine reports no carrying limit.
