◆ loadState()

std::size_t rpg_os::GameSession::loadState ( const Json &  in)
inline

Restores a state produced by saveState: replaces every owned sheet and the shared world. Each sheet is recreated from its archetype / creature record (so resource-pool bounds are rebuilt from the ruleset) and its saved living state — current pools, conditions with remaining durations, inventory, spell slots, effect timeline — is then applied, with events suppressed during the restore. Returns how many sheets were restored (a sheet whose type is absent from the ruleset is skipped).
