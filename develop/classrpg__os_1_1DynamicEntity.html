Detailed Description

A universal-mode entity: dynamic stats, resources, and conditions.

Why "dynamic"?
Unlike the generated characters, this class can represent any ruleset's entities at runtime. It is the workhorse of universal mode, and its flexibility (no recompilation for a new game) is traded against the compile-time guarantees of the specific mode.

The restriction and capability tokens contributed by the entity's active conditions and traits (deduplicated, source order). Restrictions say what the entity cannot do ("no_action", "no_bonus_action", "no_reaction", "no_move", "no_speak", "no_concentration", "no_cast", ...); capabilities say what it can do (movement / senses: "swim", "climb", "breath_water", "darkvision", ...). ◆ getEffectiveStat()

int32_t rpg_os::DynamicEntity::getEffectiveStat ( std::string_view  statId) const
inline

The effective value of statId: the raw value plus every modifier the sheet's equipped gear and active conditions contribute (through the shared modifier pipeline in core/modifier.hpp), plus any temporary stat bonuses from active effects (spells, buffs, traits).

Why not replace getStat with this?
Derived-stat formulas evaluate other stats and must not re-apply gear bonuses (an equipped +1 STR must not inflate STR-derived stats and feed back). getStat stays the raw, formula-safe value; this is the gameplay-facing number.

Why derive-on-demand instead of precomputing?

◆ setEventSink()

void rpg_os::DynamicEntity::setEventSink ( EventSink  sink)
inline

Sets (or clears, with an empty sink) the per-entity event sink. When set, the state-changing mutators emit granular events through it (OnStatChanged, OnResourceChanged, OnTempHpChanged). The engine wires this to its event bus for every entity it creates, so applications observe both engine-driven and direct sheet mutations without polling.

Why not emit from every mutator?
Conditions are announced by the engine's applyCondition / removeCondition (which fire OnConditionChanged with a rich payload and keep the timeline in sync); direct addCondition / removeCondition calls on the sheet deliberately do not emit, so the engine stays the single source of condition events.
